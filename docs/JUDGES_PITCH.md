# SchoolAir Ledger — judge pitch (60–75 seconds)

Schools do not just need another AQI number. A coordinator has to decide what to do about assembly, sports, or outdoor activity, often while checking whether the observation is current and whether nearby stations tell the same story.

SchoolAir Ledger is an evidence-to-action workflow. It keeps the source and timestamps visible, refuses to generate advice from stale or invalid evidence, and can flag material disagreement between nearby fresh stations instead of averaging it away. It compares the same evidence across four school activities, then records the coordinator's reported decision alongside the advisory and rules version.

It also includes an optional indoor-air scenario calculator, clearly labelled as a model—not a measurement—and bilingual parent-notice templates. That scenario never controls the advisory, and the safety path is deterministic rather than an LLM making health or legal decisions.

Our core contribution is accountability: **what evidence was available, what the system could and could not conclude, and what action the coordinator recorded**. The current prototype does not claim health outcomes, compliance, or live coverage until the source integration and deployment are verified.
